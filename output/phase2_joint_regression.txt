phase2_tau_variants.py — joint regression of LOEUF on τ and network connectivity
==============================================================================
Each model fits LOEUF as outcome. Predictors are z-scored before fitting so
coefficients (β) are directly comparable across predictors. VIF < 5 indicates
acceptable collinearity. Partial Spearman ρ is the rank-based analogue: it
measures the association between two predictors of LOEUF after controlling
for the other(s).
