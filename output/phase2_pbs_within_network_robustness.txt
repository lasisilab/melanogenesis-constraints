Within-network PBS — ROBUSTNESS CHECKS (combined dataset, HGDP+1KGP+SGDP)
==============================================================================
Companion to phase2_pbs_within_network_combined.py. Same inputs, same model:
outcome = WITHIN-network PBS percentile (rank among network genes with
n_sites_shared > 0, 0–100); predictors z-scored; covariates z(log1p n_sites)
and z(log10 body_length), body_length = (end - start - 20000) clipped at 1.

Three checks, corresponding to sections 3–5 of
docs/provenance_within_network_pbs.md:
  1. seed sensitivity of the permutation p-values
  2. τ / tissue_breadth collinearity (one finding or two?)
  3. covariate collinearity (the VIF the headline script never computes)

Network genes with combined-dataset PBS: 128
Permutation settings: 40 seeds × 10,000 shuffles; MEL τ also at 200,000 (seed 20261014).

# 3. COVARIATE COLLINEARITY — THE VIF THAT IS NEVER REPORTED
##############################################################################
phase2_pbs_within_network_combined.py calls variance_inflation_factor(Xv, 1):
position 1, the predictor, only. The two covariates are never checked.

  Analysis subset: n=123 (network genes with n_sites_shared > 0)
  Pearson  r(log1p n_sites, log10 body_length) = +0.961 (p=1.12e-69)
  Spearman ρ = +0.980
    z(log1p n_sites_shared)    VIF =  13.22  (covariates only)
    z(log10 body_length)       VIF =  13.22  (covariates only)

  Within the fitted headline model (predictor + both covariates):
    τ (tissue specificity)     VIF =   1.04
    z(log1p n_sites_shared)    VIF =  13.57
    z(log10 body_length)       VIF =  13.66

  The predictor's own VIF is low, so the τ estimate is not directly
  destabilised. The covariate adjustment itself is not stable: the two
  covariates are near-duplicates and come out large and offsetting.
  POSTER_METHODS_GUIDE.md's claim that 'all VIFs < 1.5' describes only the
  predictor position and is not supportable as written.


==============================================================================
SUMMARY
==============================================================================
  1. Of the four headline tests, three are stable across all 40 seeds.
     MEL τ (β=+5.28) is not: mean perm p = 0.0490, 62% of seeds below 0.05.
     At 200,000 permutations it lands at p = 0.04888.
  2. τ and tissue_breadth correlate at r = -0.890; in the joint model both
     null out at VIF ≈ 5. They are one finding, not two.
  3. The two covariates correlate at r = +0.961, VIF ≈ 13.2 each.
     The headline script never computes these.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAVEATS:
  • This script changes nothing about the headline result; it characterises it.
  • The AFR result is unaffected by all three checks.
  • Permutation betas use the closed-form pinv solution rather than refitting
    OLS; this is algebraically identical, not an approximation.
  • A permutation p near the threshold is a statement about the test, not about
    the biology. MEL rests on n=47 samples (30 HGDP + 17 SGDP).
  • PBS is a frequency-differentiation statistic, NOT proof of selection.
